# Material catalogue

The material catalogue is where you store and manage the materials you use in your estimates. Keeping your catalogue up to date ensures your estimates are accurate and consistent.

## Viewing materials

The main table shows all the materials in your catalogue. Each row displays the material's details, including its **Material** name and code, **Category**, **Specification**, **Unit**, whether it is **Active**, its default **Rate**, and when it was last **Updated**.

You can use the **Search materials...** bar at the top of the table to quickly find specific items by name, code, category, specification, or unit.

## Adding a material

Click the **Create Material** button to add a new item to your catalogue. This will open a window where you can enter the material's details.

## Managing materials

Click the **More Options** button (the three dots) on the right side of any row to see actions for that material. From this menu, you can:

- **Edit Material:** Change the details of an existing material.
- **Duplicate Material:** Create a copy of the material. This is useful for adding similar items without typing everything again.
- **Delete Material:** Remove the material from your catalogue.

## Bulk actions

You can select one or more materials using the checkboxes on the left side of the table. Once selected, a toolbar will appear at the bottom of the screen. From here, you can:

- **Clear:** Deselect all chosen materials.
- **Delete Materials:** Permanently remove all selected materials from your catalogue at once.

## Empty list

If your catalogue is empty, you can add new items individually or use the import feature to bring in existing materials from a spreadsheet.
