Kwantflow uses different record types for different kinds of work.

- RFQs are job records.
- Assets are estimating bundles inside those job records.
- Estimates are working costing records inside an asset.
- Companies are shared business records used across many RFQs.
- Setup records such as rate cards, templates, presets, and classifications are shared workspace references.

If a user asks whether two things are related, answer in business terms first and explain where each record sits in the normal workflow.
