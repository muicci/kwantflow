RFQs (Requests for Quotation) are the top-level jobs in Kwantflow. The RFQ list is your main dashboard for tracking every enquiry that comes into the shop.

### The RFQ List

The main table shows key details for every job:

- **RFQ Number**: The unique identifier for the job (e.g. RFQ-1001).
- **Company**: The customer company and the specific person who requested the quote.
- **Project Name**: The descriptive name of the project or part.
- **Priority**: How urgent the job is (Low, Medium, High, or Critical).
- **Status**: Where the job is in your workflow (e.g. Open, Quoted, Won, Lost).
- **Files**: The number of files attached to the RFQ for review.
- **Submission Deadline**: When the quote is due. Critical deadlines are highlighted in red.

### Managing RFQs

Click the **...** button at the end of any row to open the actions menu:

- **Edit RFQ**: Update job details like the RFQ number, company, due date, or notes.
- **Open Workspace**: Jump into the project to start reviewing files and building an estimate.
- **Duplicate RFQ**: Create a copy of the job.
- **Archive RFQ**: Move completed or cancelled jobs out of your active list.

### Organising your view

You can switch between **Active**, **Archived**, and **All** RFQs using the selector at the top of the list.

- **Reordering**: Click and drag the handle at the left of any row to manually reorder your active RFQs. Note that manual reordering is disabled if you have clicked a column header to sort the list.
- **Bulk Actions**: Select multiple RFQs using the checkboxes on the left to archive, restore, or delete them all at once.
