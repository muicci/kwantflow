---

Cost presets are reusable bundles of cost components.

They are useful when the same group of cost items appears repeatedly across estimates. Instead of rebuilding that structure every time, users can start from a preset and then adjust it to suit the job.

If a user asks when to use a preset, the answer is when they want speed and consistency for repeated pricing patterns.
