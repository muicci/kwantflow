Kwantflow uses a sidebar and tabs to help you organise your work.

### The sidebar

The sidebar is your main tool for moving around the app. It has several panels that you can switch between:

- **Explorer**: Browse through your current project files and structure.
- **Projects**: View and manage your RFQs and active jobs.
- **Pages**: Access your setup pages, like materials, rates, and classification systems.
- **Mail**: Review project emails and communications.

You can also use the bottom menu in the sidebar to open your user profile, Settings, and workspace options.

### Working with tabs

Kwantflow uses tabs to keep your active work open while you look at other things. This means you can keep a quote open while you switch over to look at your material catalogue.

- Click a tab to switch to that work.
- You can drag tabs left and right to reorganise them.
- Click the close icon on a tab when you finish with it.

If you close all tabs, Kwantflow opens a new default home tab so you never lose your place.
