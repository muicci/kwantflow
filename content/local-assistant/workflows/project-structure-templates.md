Project structure templates help users reuse a known breakdown on similar work.

They are useful when the business regularly estimates jobs that share the same internal structure, such as repeated phases, trades, or areas.

If a user asks when to use a structure template, the answer is when they want a repeatable starting structure before fine-tuning the current job.
