---

AI drafting helps prepare a working estimate. It does not replace estimator review.

The assistant can use supported evidence from the current asset to prepare a draft estimate, but the result still needs a human to review it, edit it, and save it.

AI output should stay explainable to the user:

- evidence and issues should remain visible
- weak or ambiguous inputs should stay unresolved instead of being guessed
- final totals and commercial decisions still belong to the normal estimate and quote workflow

If a user asks what AI does, explain it as a drafting assist inside the normal estimating workflow, not as an automatic final answer.
