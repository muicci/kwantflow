Asset estimates are the working inputs for quote preparation.

Once the relevant asset estimates are ready, users prepare quote content from the project quote surface. That is where quote options, revisions, pricing presentation, and PDF output come together.

Kwantflow keeps estimate editing and quote preparation related but separate:

- the asset estimate is where scope and cost detail are prepared
- the quote surface is where the commercial output is assembled

If a user asks where totals come from, explain that quote output rolls up from the estimate data that has already been prepared in the project.
