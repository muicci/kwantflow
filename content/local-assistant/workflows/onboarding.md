When you open Kwantflow for the first time, the app guides you through setting up your local workspace.

### 1. Workspace setup

You need to name and configure your first workspace. The **workspace setup** process ensures your device is ready for local-first estimating.

- **Workspace name**: Give your workspace a name (like your company name). This is how it will appear in your workspace list. Click **Continue** when you are ready.
- **Business profile**: Enter your business details, including your country, business name, and contact information. Kwantflow uses these details for quotes and documents. You will also need to provide your business identifier (such as an **ABN** in Australia or **NZBN** in New Zealand). Select your **Role** and **Industry** from the lists and click **Create workspace**.

### 2. Optional setup import

If you have existing business data in spreadsheets, you can use the **optional setup import** step to bring in your data now. This includes company lists, labour rates, and cost codes.

- Select **Choose setup spreadsheet** to upload a **CSV** or **XLSX** file.
- The import wizard helps you match your spreadsheet columns to Kwantflow fields.
- You can also **Skip for now** and add this data later in the **Settings** menu.

### 3. Optional Local AI

The final step is the **optional Local AI** configuration. This enables on-device drafting and local help features.

- Click **Download Kwantflow AI** to start the download. It is a large file, so it may take a few minutes depending on your internet speed.
- You can continue to **Enter workspace** while the download runs in the background, or skip this step and set it up later.

Once finished, you will arrive at the **RFQ home** where you can start managing your projects.
