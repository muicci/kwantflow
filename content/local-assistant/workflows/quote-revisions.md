title: Quote revisions
kind: help
summary: Quote revisions help users capture changes to quote output over time without losing the current pricing context.
keywords: quote revision, revisions, revise quote, version, versioning, quote history
---

Quote revisions are used when the quote output changes and the user needs a clear history of that change.

They help keep the commercial output organised over time instead of overwriting everything with no record of what changed.

If a user asks when to use a quote revision, the answer is when they need to issue or track a new version of the quote rather than just keep editing the same working view.
