Companies and workspace setup records are shared reference data.

They are different from RFQs and assets because they are reused across many jobs instead of belonging to one job only.

Examples of shared workspace data include:

- company records
- material catalogues
- labour, equipment, and subcontract rates
- cost presets
- estimate templates
- classification systems and cost codes

If a user asks why setup records matter, explain that they make estimating faster and more consistent across projects.
