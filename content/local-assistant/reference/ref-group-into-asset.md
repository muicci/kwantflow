# What does Group into Asset do?

In Kwantflow, you can organise multiple related files into a single item by using the **Create Asset** feature. This is sometimes called a "Scope Item".

When you have a project with many files—such as a 3D model, a set of drawings, and some specification documents—that all relate to the same physical object, grouping them together helps keep your estimate clean and organised.

## How to group files

1. Open a project to see your files in the **Context** area.
2. Select the files you want to group by checking their selection boxes. You can see how many files are selected in the corner.
3. Click the **Create Asset** button to open the grouping window.
4. Give your new asset a clear, descriptive name (for example, "Bracket Assembly").
5. Click **Create Scope Item**.

Your newly created asset will now appear in the **Assets** list, ready for you to open and build its estimate.

## How to ungroup an asset

If you need to separate the files later, you can ungroup the asset.

1. Find the asset in the **Assets** list.
2. Click the **More actions** button (the three dots) next to the asset.
3. Select **Ungroup Asset**.

The files will be returned to the **Context** area for review. **Please note:** Ungrouping an asset will permanently delete any estimate data you have built for that specific asset.
