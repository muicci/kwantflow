# Kwantflow name and logo

The Kwantflow **source code** is licensed under the [GNU GPL v3.0 only](LICENSE). You may use,
modify and redistribute it under those terms.

The GPL covers code, not names. The **"Kwantflow" name and logo** (including the icons in
`assets/brand/` and the "Kwantflow Open CAD" font family name) identify this project. To avoid
confusing users:

- **You may** say that your project is "based on Kwantflow" or "a fork of Kwantflow", and use
  the name to refer to this project accurately.
- **You may** build and share unmodified builds of an official release under the Kwantflow name.
- **Please do not** distribute a modified build under the Kwantflow name or logo, or in a way
  that suggests it is the official project. Give your fork its own name, icon and bundle
  identifier (`identifier` in `src-tauri/tauri.conf.json`), and rename the derived font family
  as required by its SIL Open Font License.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
